Information design

A wordless instruction manual

Turning a set of technical drawings of a device into a 12-step picture-only guide to setting it up, using it and packing it away.

Context
English 586, Iowa State, 2026
My role
Individual project: designed the 12-step manual from the drawings
Starting point
Product drawings: top, side and front views and a carrying case
Tool
Adobe Illustrator

The brief

We were given static drawings of a portable device from several angles, plus its carrying case. The task was to redesign them into instructions that explain how to use the device.

Design approach

  • A clear sequence. Twelve numbered panels run from lifting the device out of its case to packing it back in.
  • Hands show the action. Drawn hands, arrows and rotation marks show what to press, turn or open.
  • Zoom circles for detail. Small parts such as buttons and dials are enlarged in circles linked to their place on the device.
  • Safety first. A warning panel sits at the center of the first page.
  • Reused views. The original drawings are reused at each step, so the device always looks the same.
