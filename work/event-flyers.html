Information design

Career talk flyers for SAPC

Two event flyers for the Society for the Advancement of Professional Communication at Iowa State, designed from scratch.

Context
SAPC events, Iowa State, Spring 2026
My role
Designed flyer 1 on my own; did most of the design on flyer 2 with a peer
Format
Letter-size print and digital

The brief

SAPC hosts career talks with technical communication alumni. Each flyer needed to catch attention on a bulletin board and answer the basics at a glance: who is speaking, when, where, and why a student should come.

Design approach

  • Iowa State's red and gold, used as large blocks of color so the flyers stand out.
  • Oversized type for “Career Talk” and the speaker's name, read first from a distance.
  • The date, time and room grouped together, with the speaker's background and a reason to attend underneath.
  • A shared layout, so the two flyers read as one series.
