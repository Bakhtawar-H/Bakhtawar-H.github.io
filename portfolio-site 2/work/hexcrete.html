Hexcrete research website

Moving a civil engineering professor's research site to Iowa State's official web template, and improving it on the way.

Context
Department of Civil, Construction and Environmental Engineering, Iowa State
Timeline
Summer 2026
My role
Content migration, editing, image selection, page structure
Constraint
Iowa State web template

The project

Hexcrete is a tower technology for wind turbines at hub heights of 120 meters and above, built from modular high-strength concrete parts that avoid the transportation limits of tall steel towers. The professor leading the research needed the content of his old website moved to a new site that follows Iowa State's official template.

What I did

  • Migrated the content from the old site into the university template's layouts and components.
  • Edited the text as I moved it.
  • Chose new and better images where the old ones were weak.
  • Reorganized the pages so the project overview, background and wind energy material are easier to follow.

Working inside a university template meant making content decisions within fixed layouts, the same constraint most institutional and corporate web work comes with.
